About This Table

This table is based on the Social Security Administration's Actuarial Life Table (2024). It shows the average remaining years of life expectancy for individuals at different ages.

How to read this table: Find your current age in the "Current Age" column, then look at the corresponding "Remaining Life Expectancy" to see the average number of years you can expect to live from that age.

Note: These are statistical averages. Individual life expectancy can vary based on health, lifestyle, genetics, and other factors.

Understanding the Data

  • Current Age: The person's age today
  • Remaining Life Expectancy: The average number of additional years a person of this age is expected to live
  • Expected Age at Death: Current age + remaining life expectancy

Example: A 65-year-old male has a life expectancy of 17.8 more years, meaning they are expected to live to approximately age 82.8.
