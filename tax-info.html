Important: Short-term capital gains do not receive preferential tax treatment and are taxed as ordinary income, which can result in rates as high as 37%.

Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)

Additional 3.8% tax on investment income for high earners

What is NIIT?

The Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT) is a 3.8% tax that applies to investment income of individuals, estates, and trusts that have income above statutory threshold amounts.

Who Pays NIIT?

NIIT applies when your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) exceeds:

  • $250,000 for Married Filing Jointly
  • $200,000 for Single or Head of Household
  • $125,000 for Married Filing Separately

What Income is Subject to NIIT?

  • Capital gains from the sale of stocks, bonds, real estate
  • Interest and dividend income
  • Rental and royalty income
  • Non-qualified annuities
  • Income from businesses involved in trading of financial instruments or commodities

NIIT Calculation Example

Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI): $500,000
Filing Status Threshold (Single): $200,000
Amount Over Threshold: $300,000
Net Investment Income: $250,000
Lesser of the two: $250,000
NIIT @ 3.8%: $9,500
